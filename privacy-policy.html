Privacy Policy

Your data matters. This policy explains what the Built by Kris extensions collect, how it is used, and how to get in touch.

Overview

The Chrome extensions listed on builtbykris.com are designed to make your browser work better for you. I believe browser tools should be helpful, transparent, and respect your privacy by default.

Information I collect

  • Extension usage data: Anonymous metrics such as feature usage, errors, and installed version may be collected to improve reliability and prioritize fixes.
  • Content you provide: Some extensions process text or page content you explicitly choose to interact with (for example, a contract you upload or a post you draft). This content is used only to deliver the extension’s feature and is not stored permanently unless the feature clearly requires it.
  • No selling of data: I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

How I use information

  • To operate and improve the extensions.
  • To diagnose bugs and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate important updates when you contact support.

Third-party services

Some extensions may rely on third-party APIs or cloud services to generate text, summarize content, or process data. Those services have their own privacy policies and only receive the information you explicitly choose to send through the extension.

Your choices

  • You can uninstall any extension at any time through Chrome’s extension manager.
  • You can clear extension data or disable permissions from Chrome settings.
  • You may contact me with questions, deletion requests, or feedback.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. I will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email support@builtbykris.com.

Effective date: 30 July 2026
