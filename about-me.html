How I think

Technology should return time, not consume it.

I’m fascinated by what happens when clear operational thinking meets modern AI and approachable code. My work sits at that intersection: understanding how people actually work, finding the friction that has become “normal,” and building a simpler path through it.

I’m comfortable moving between strategy and implementation. That can mean shaping an automation roadmap, interrogating a process with data, prototyping a browser extension, building a pricing calculator, improving reporting, or using AI to turn an awkward manual task into a dependable workflow.

The goal is never automation for its own sake. It is less rework, fewer avoidable clicks, clearer decisions, and more room for people to do work that benefits from judgment, creativity, and human connection.
